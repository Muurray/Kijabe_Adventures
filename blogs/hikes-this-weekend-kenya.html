Introduction

Searching for hikes this weekend in Kenya? A weekend on the trail is a simple way to escape the city, enjoy fresh air and explore Kenya's hills, forests, escarpments and countryside.

For hikers around Nairobi and the surrounding region, Kijabe Hills offers a convenient guided hiking experience with scenic Great Rift Valley views, countryside trails and opportunities to meet other outdoor enthusiasts.

Looking for Hikes This Weekend?

Weekend hikes can range from short nature walks to longer mountain and escarpment adventures. Before choosing a route, consider your fitness level, available time, weather conditions and whether you want to hike independently or with a group.

  • Scenic hill and escarpment hikes
  • Forest and nature walks
  • Waterfall and river-trail experiences
  • Guided group hikes
  • Private hikes for couples, families and organizations

For current dates, meeting points and availability, check our Upcoming Hiking Events page.

Why Consider Kijabe Hills?

Kijabe Hills is located in Kijabe, Kenya, along the eastern edge of the Great Rift Valley. Its elevated landscape provides wide views across the surrounding countryside and Rift Valley.

A guided Kijabe Hills hike can combine scenic viewpoints, forest and countryside trails, fresh highland air and opportunities for outdoor photography. The exact route and trail conditions can vary, so hikers should follow current guidance from local guides.

Hiking Groups in Kenya

If you prefer not to hike alone, joining a hiking group in Kenya can make the experience more social and easier to organize. Group hikes can bring together beginners, experienced hikers, couples, friends and visitors who want to discover new trails.

Group hiking may also simplify route planning, transport coordination, safety communication and meeting-point arrangements. Kijabe Adventures offers scheduled group hikes as well as private hiking experiences.

What to Expect on a Guided Kijabe Hills Hike

Scenic viewpoints

Expect open landscapes and viewpoints across the Rift Valley and surrounding countryside.

Nature and countryside trails

Depending on the route, the experience may include forest, grassland, rocky sections and countryside paths.

A social outdoor experience

Group hikes provide an opportunity to meet other hikers while exploring with local guides.

Changing trail conditions

Weather can affect hiking conditions. Always follow current instructions from the guides and avoid unsafe routes.

What to Carry for a Weekend Hike

  • At least 2 litres of drinking water
  • Comfortable hiking shoes or boots
  • Light snacks
  • Hat or cap
  • Sunscreen
  • Light rain jacket
  • Fully charged phone and power bank
  • Small backpack
