Explore the Scenic Kijabe Hills

Located approximately one hour from Nairobi, Kijabe Hills offers one of Kenya's most rewarding hiking experiences. Rolling green hills, panoramic escarpment views, indigenous forests, cool mountain air, and spectacular viewpoints make it a favourite destination for both beginner and experienced hikers.

Whether you're looking for a peaceful nature walk or a challenging day hike, Kijabe Hills has trails suitable for every fitness level.

Discover the Beauty of Kijabe Forest

Kijabe Forest forms part of the escarpment ecosystem overlooking the Great Rift Valley. The forest is home to indigenous trees, birds, butterflies, monkeys, and numerous hiking trails that provide a peaceful escape from Nairobi's busy city life.

Walking with a local guide helps visitors identify native tree species, birdlife, medicinal plants, and hidden viewpoints while ensuring they stay on safe trails.

Explore Historic Old Kijabe Township

Beyond its natural beauty, Kijabe is rich in history. Old Kijabe Township played an important role during the construction of the Uganda Railway and still contains historical landmarks that tell the story of Kenya's colonial railway era.

Our guided hikes include fascinating stories about the railway, early settlers, historic churches, schools, and the development of one of Kenya's oldest hill settlements.

Breathtaking Great Rift Valley Viewpoints

Few hiking destinations near Nairobi offer views comparable to Kijabe. From elevated ridges you can admire the Great Rift Valley stretching for miles, dramatic escarpments, rolling hills, forests, and beautiful sunsets.

Photographers particularly enjoy sunrise and golden hour when the landscape is illuminated with warm colours across the valley.

Wildlife and Birdwatching

Although Kijabe is not a national park, hikers frequently encounter monkeys, colourful bird species, butterflies, reptiles, and numerous indigenous plants. The surrounding forests provide excellent birdwatching opportunities throughout the year.

Photography Opportunities

Kijabe Hills is one of the best places near Nairobi for landscape photography. Visitors enjoy photographing dramatic escarpments, railway viewpoints, misty forests, wildlife, sunsets, and panoramic Rift Valley scenery.

Our local guides know the best photography spots and ideal times of day for unforgettable images.

Getting to Kijabe from Nairobi

Kijabe is located about one hour from Nairobi via the Nairobi–Nakuru Highway. Visitors can travel using private vehicles, hired transport, or join one of our organised hiking trips, which include convenient transport options from Nairobi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hike Kijabe Hills without a guide?

Yes, but hiring a local guide is highly recommended. A guide can help you navigate the trails, share local history, point out landmarks, and improve your overall hiking experience.

How long does a guided hike take?

Most guided hikes last between 3 and 6 hours depending on the trail, pace, and stops for sightseeing or photography.

Is Kijabe suitable for beginners?

Yes. Several trails around Kijabe are beginner-friendly, while others provide more challenging options for experienced hikers.

Can families hire a guide?

Absolutely. We offer family-friendly guided walks, private hikes, and customized experiences for visitors of all ages.

How do I book a local guide?

You can book online through our Upcoming Hikes page, request a private guided hike, or contact Kijabe Adventures directly via WhatsApp.
