OUR HOSPITALITY COLLABORATION

Kijabe Adventures × Cafe Ubuntu

More than a hiking experience. Discover a complete outdoor experience combining adventure, hospitality, dining, team building and exclusive benefits for our members.

Representatives of Kijabe Adventures and Cafe Ubuntu during a partnership discussion in Maai Mahiu.

Bringing Adventure and Hospitality Together

At Kijabe Adventures, we believe that an unforgettable outdoor experience goes beyond the hiking trail. It is also about the people we meet, the places we discover and the experiences we share along the way.

Through our collaboration with Cafe Ubuntu, Maai Mahiu, we are working towards making our hiking experiences even more convenient and enjoyable by bringing together outdoor adventure, hospitality, dining and group activities.

Whether you are joining us for a group hike, a private adventure, a corporate outing or a team-building experience, Cafe Ubuntu offers an additional hospitality option for selected Kijabe Adventures experiences.

Visit the official Ubuntu Life website to learn more about their business.

What This Collaboration Offers Our Hikers

Convenient Meeting Point

Cafe Ubuntu can serve as a designated meeting point for selected Kijabe Adventures hiking trips and group experiences passing through Maai Mahiu.

Team Building & Group Activities

Explore opportunities for group activities, corporate team building and social experiences in a welcoming environment.

Post-Hike Dining

After exploring Kenya's trails, groups can enjoy meals, refreshments and time to relax and connect at Cafe Ubuntu.

Exclusive Member Discounts

Kijabe Adventures members can present their valid membership cards at Cafe Ubuntu to access special discounts on eligible meals and products.

Enjoy More With Your Kijabe Membership

Being part of the Kijabe Adventures community comes with additional experiences beyond the trails.

Through our collaboration with Cafe Ubuntu, eligible Kijabe Adventures members can enjoy exclusive discounts on meals and products purchased at the café.

How to enjoy your member benefits

  • Bring your Kijabe Adventures membership card.
  • Present it to the Cafe Ubuntu team before payment.
  • Ask for the applicable Kijabe Adventures member discount.
  • Enjoy your meal or purchase with your membership benefits.

Remember: Always present your membership card before completing your purchase.
