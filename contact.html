HAVE QUESTIONS?

Hiking & Booking Questions

Do I need previous hiking experience?

No. We offer hiking experiences suitable for beginners as well as more experienced hikers. Let us know your experience level when booking so we can help you choose an appropriate hike.

What should I bring on a hike?

Bring enough drinking water, comfortable hiking shoes, sunscreen, weather-appropriate clothing and a light jacket. Depending on the hike, additional items may be recommended.

How do I secure my hiking spot?

After availability is confirmed, a 30% booking fee is required to secure your place. The remaining balance can be paid on the hike day or before.

Can I book a private group hike?

Yes. We organize private hiking experiences for friends, families, teams, schools and organizations. Visit our private group hiking page or contact us directly for assistance.

Can children participate?

Children may be able to participate depending on the specific trail, distance and difficulty level. Contact us before booking so we can recommend suitable options.

What happens if the weather changes?

Hiking conditions can change, particularly during rainy periods. We prioritize participant safety and will communicate with hikers if weather conditions require a change to the hiking plan.

Where do we meet for the hike?

Meeting points vary depending on the hiking destination. Once your booking is confirmed, we will provide the relevant meeting point, timing and directions.

Can I ask about a hike that is not listed?

Yes. If you have a specific trail, destination or outdoor experience in mind, contact us and we'll let you know what options are available.
