Our Story

From Kijabe Hills to the trails of Kenya

Kijabe Adventures was built from a simple love for the outdoors: the feeling of reaching a summit, walking through a forest, discovering a new trail and sharing the experience with others.

While Kijabe Hills and Kijabe Forest remain our home base, our adventures extend far beyond Kijabe. We guide hikers across some of Kenya's most spectacular landscapes, including Mount Longonot, Ngong Hills, Mount Margaret, the Aberdares, Naivasha and the Great Rift Valley escarpments.

Our approach combines local knowledge, responsible tourism, careful planning and genuine hospitality to create experiences that are memorable without losing sight of safety or conservation.

The People Behind the Adventures

Meet your guides

Behind every well-run adventure is a team that cares about the details — from the route and logistics to your experience on the trail.

Martin Muuri

Co-Founder, Logistics & Safety Coordinator

Martin is the logistical force behind Kijabe Adventures. He coordinates routes, transportation, equipment and group logistics while helping ensure every expedition is carefully planned and responsibly managed.

With a background in programming and operations, Martin brings a practical, detail-oriented approach to creating smooth and enjoyable outdoor experiences.

Wilson Kuria

Co-Founder, Lead Guide & Operations Director

Wilson oversees hiking expeditions and brings extensive experience navigating Kenya's diverse mountain and trail environments.

As a lead mountain guide with more than 10 years of trail experience, he focuses on route leadership, group safety and making every adventure memorable.
