# Gallery Page Transformation Summary

## Overview
The Kijabe Adventures Gallery page has been completely transformed from a simple photo gallery into a **comprehensive SEO and conversion-focused page** designed to rank for hiking-related searches and guide visitors toward bookings.

---

## 🎯 Key Improvements

### 1. **Enhanced Structured Data (Schema.org)**
- **Main Page Schema**: Upgraded to `CollectionPage` with `ImageGallery` component
- **Breadcrumb Navigation**: Added for improved SEO and user navigation
- **Video Object**: Enhanced with additional keywords, author info, and family-friendly flag
- **Related Experiences List**: Added ItemList schema for Kijabe Hills, Ngong Hills, and Mt Longonot
- **Semantic Markup**: Full address and contact details included in structured data

**SEO Impact**: Better indexing by Google, rich snippets in search results, improved click-through rates.

---

### 2. **Featured Hiking Experiences Section** (NEW)
A prominent section highlighting 4 major hiking destinations:

#### 4 Featured Hikes
1. **Kijabe Hills**
   - Duration: 4-6 hours | Difficulty: Intermediate
   - Focus: Rift Valley views, forest trails, cultural sites
   - CTA: "Book a Kijabe Hills Hike"

2. **Ngong Hills**
   - Duration: 2-4 hours | Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate
   - Focus: Iconic Nairobi peaks, 360° views
   - CTA: "Explore Ngong Hills"

3. **Mt Longonot**
   - Duration: 3-4 hours | Difficulty: Intermediate
   - Focus: Volcanic crater, crater lake, Rift Valley panoramas

4. **Forest & Nature Trails**
   - Variety of difficulty levels

- Clear hiking options with difficulty/duration info
