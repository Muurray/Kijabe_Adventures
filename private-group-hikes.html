Guided Private Hiking Tours Across Kenya

Kijabe Adventures organizes private group hikes across Kenya for corporate teams, schools, churches, families, friends and travel groups seeking memorable outdoor experiences.

Our most popular hiking destinations include Kijabe Hills (Kijabe River Walk Trails, Kiharu Waterfalls, and Kijabe Railway Trail), as well as Ngong Hills, Mt Longonot and Karura Forest.

Whether you want a beginner-friendly nature walk, a scenic waterfall adventure, or a challenging mountain hike, we provide experienced local guides, organized logistics and customized hiking packages.

Private Hiking Packages for Every Group

Couple Hiking Adventures

Enjoy a scenic outdoor date, a relaxed nature walk or a private hiking adventure together. Couple packages can be planned around photography stops, viewpoints and the preferred activity level.

Family Nature Walks

Spend time outdoors with family through suitable guided walks, nature observation and scenic trails. Route difficulty, duration and age suitability should be agreed before booking.

Corporate Team-Building Hikes

Private Hike Prices and Group Discounts

The cost of a private hiking trip depends on the activity, number of participants, duration, destination, guide requirements and optional transport. Larger groups may qualify for a lower per-person guiding rate when staffing, route capacity and safety requirements allow.

Use the calculator above to estimate your selected package for the destinations and group details shown. Camping, specialist bird-watching, filming, security arrangements, third-party entry fees and multi-day trips may require a customised quotation. Confirm the final inclusions and total before making payment.

Planning a larger group? For school trips, corporate events, church groups or parties with more than 30 participants, contact us early so that guide numbers, transport, permissions and safety arrangements can be reviewed.

Transport, Preparation and What to Expect

When requesting a private hike, indicate whether your group will arrange its own transport to the meeting point or would like to discuss transport options. Arranging your own transport may reduce the overall cost, depending on the destination and any parking or access charges.

Wear comfortable walking shoes and weather-appropriate clothing. Bring drinking water, snacks or meals appropriate to the trip, sun protection and any personal medication you may need. Longer hikes and camping trips may require additional equipment and preparation instructions.

Before departure, confirm the meeting point, start time, expected duration, route difficulty, weather considerations, payment terms and what the package includes. Do not assume park entry, meals, camping equipment, insurance or transport are included unless they are expressly listed in your confirmed booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private group hike cost in Kenya?

The price depends on your group size, activity, destination, duration and transport requirements. Use the booking calculator for the destinations and group details shown or contact Kijabe Adventures for a quotation for specialist activities and multi-day trips.

Can I book a private hike for two people?

Yes. You can request a private hike for a couple or a small group. Share your preferred date, destination and activity so your itinerary and final price can be confirmed.

Do you organise corporate and school hiking trips?

You can request customised outdoor experiences for corporate teams, schools, churches and families. The itinerary, group supervision, guide requirements and safety arrangements depend on the group.

Can we arrange our own transport to the meeting point?

Yes, where the selected destination permits it. Arranging your own transport may reduce the total cost. Confirm the exact meeting point, parking charges and access arrangements before travelling.

Can I book camping, bird-watching or a waterfall trip?

Yes. These activities and the locations advertised on this page are available to book. We will plan the itinerary and confirm any required access permissions, guide arrangements, equipment and additional fees.

When is my private hike booking confirmed?

Your private hike booking is confirmed after your 30% booking fee or full payment has been received.

When will I receive the meeting point details?

The meeting point or arranged pick-up point details are shared after your 30% booking fee has been received.

When should I pay the remaining balance?

If you paid the 30% booking fee, the remaining balance must be paid after you meet your assigned guides and before the hike begins.

Can I pay the full amount in advance?

Yes. You can pay the full booking amount in advance instead of paying the 30% booking fee and clearing the balance on the day of the hike.

Are the hikes beginner friendly?

Yes. We offer beginner-friendly hikes, moderate hikes and advanced adventure hikes tailored to your group's fitness level.

How far in advance should I book a private group hike?

We recommend booking at least 3–7 days in advance to confirm guide availability and logistics. For larger groups (20+), 2 weeks notice is ideal.
