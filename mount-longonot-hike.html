Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining the Mount Longonot hiking adventure.

🏔️ How difficult is Mount Longonot?

Mount Longonot is a moderate to challenging hiking destination with steep sections, uneven terrain and sustained uphill sections. Participants should have reasonable fitness.

⏱️ How long does the hike take?

The duration depends on hiking pace, rest stops, weather and route conditions. Participants should allow several hours for the complete guided experience.

🥾 Can beginners join?

Beginners with reasonable fitness can participate, but Mount Longonot is more physically demanding than an easy nature walk. Participants should prepare for steep climbs and uneven terrain.

🎒 What should I carry?

Carry sufficient drinking water, snacks, comfortable hiking shoes, sunscreen, a hat, a light rain jacket, a fully charged phone and any personal medication you require.

🎟️ How do I book?

Complete the booking calculator, review the required booking fee, pay through the provided M-Pesa Till Number and send the payment confirmation through WhatsApp.

💳 Can I pay on the hiking day?

A 30% booking fee is required to reserve your place. The remaining balance may be paid before or on the hiking day unless otherwise communicated.

🚐 Is transport available?

Transport options may be available depending on the event. Contact Kijabe Adventures for the available options for the selected hike.

📍 Where is the meeting point?

For safety and easier coordination, the exact meeting location is shared privately with confirmed participants through WhatsApp after booking.

👨‍👩‍👧 Can children participate?

Children may participate when accompanied by a responsible adult, subject to the hiking route requirements and conditions.

🌧️ What happens if it rains?

Light rain may not necessarily stop the hike. If severe weather or unsafe trail conditions occur, the event may be postponed and participants will be notified.

📷 Can I take photographs?

Yes. Photography is encouraged. Participants should remain aware of the terrain and follow guide instructions when taking photographs.

👥 Do you organize private hikes?

Yes. Kijabe Adventures organizes private hiking experiences for families, schools, corporate organizations, churches and other groups.

📱 How can I contact Kijabe Adventures?

You can contact Kijabe Adventures through WhatsApp using the booking and contact buttons on this page.
