Featured Hiking Experiences

Explore our most popular Kenya hiking destinations with real photos and guided adventure details.

Kijabe Hills

Experience the iconic Kijabe Hills with views of the Great Rift Valley and historic Old Kijabe Township. Perfect for intermediate to advanced hikers seeking scenic forest trails and panoramic Kenya vistas.

  • Duration: 4-6 hours
  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Highlights: Rift Valley views, forest trails, cultural sites
Book a Kijabe Hills Hike

Ngong Hills

Nairobi's most iconic hiking destination. The famous Ngong Hills ridgeline offers spectacular 360-degree views of Nairobi, the surrounding countryside, and the Great Rift Valley floor. Ideal for all fitness levels.

  • Duration: 2-4 hours
  • Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate
  • Highlights: Ridgeline views, accessible peaks, wildlife encounters
Explore Ngong Hills

Mt Longonot

An extinct volcano crater hike in the Great Rift Valley. Mt Longonot rewards hikers with panoramic crater views, volcanic landscape, and year-round excellent hiking conditions. A must-do for Kenya hiking enthusiasts.

  • Duration: 3-4 hours
  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Highlights: Volcanic crater, crater lake, Rift Valley panoramas
Schedule Mt Longonot Trek
