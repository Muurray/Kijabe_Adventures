Trail Safety & Hiking Tips

Your safety is our top priority. Use this checklist to prepare well and hike responsibly.

Safety Essentials

Check Weather

Review forecasts before you travel. Avoid exposed ridges and river crossings during storms or heavy rain.

Stay on Trails

Stick to marked routes to protect nature and reduce injury risk. If in doubt, ask your guide before deviating.

Hydrate & Fuel

Carry 1–2L water (more for hot days). Bring light snacks to keep energy steady.

Be Prepared

Bring a basic first-aid kit and personal medication. Tell someone your plan and expected return time.

How We Keep Every Hike Safe

At Kijabe Adventures, the safety of every participant comes before anything else. Whether you're joining your very first hike or you're an experienced outdoor enthusiast, our team follows carefully planned safety procedures to ensure everyone enjoys the adventure with confidence.

Experienced Guides

Every hike is led by knowledgeable local guides who are familiar with the terrain, trail conditions, weather patterns, and emergency procedures. Our guides maintain a comfortable pace and ensure no hiker is left behind.

Group Management

We keep the group together throughout the hike and assign lead and sweep guides on larger trips. Regular head counts are conducted during rest stops and at major trail junctions.

Weather Monitoring

Before every hike we assess weather forecasts and trail conditions. If heavy rain, strong winds, or other hazards are expected, we postpone or adjust the route to protect participants.

Emergency Preparedness

Our guides carry first-aid supplies and know how to respond to common hiking emergencies. We also maintain communication whenever network coverage is available and have evacuation plans for each hiking destination.

Trail Briefing

Before setting off, every participant receives a safety briefing covering trail difficulty, expected hiking time, hydration, environmental conservation, and emergency procedures.

Looking After Every Hiker

We encourage hikers to move at a comfortable pace and take breaks whenever necessary. Our guides regularly check on participants and provide assistance to anyone feeling tired or uncomfortable during the hike.

Your adventure should be memorable for the right reasons. Our commitment to preparation, responsible guiding, and respect for nature allows you to focus on enjoying Kenya's beautiful landscapes while we take care of your safety.

Our Commitment to Responsible Hiking

Every hike is an opportunity to protect Kenya's beautiful landscapes. We encourage all participants to follow the Leave No Trace principles by respecting wildlife, staying on designated trails, and carrying all waste back for proper disposal.

  • 🌿 Leave nature exactly as you found it.
  • 🚯 Carry all litter back with you.
  • 🐦 Respect wildlife and vegetation.
  • 🔥 Avoid lighting fires unless permitted.
  • 🥾 Stay on established trails.

What to Carry

  • Good shoes (trail/running shoes or boots)
  • Water (1–2L), light snacks
  • Hat, sunscreen, rain jacket (weather changes fast)
  • Warm layer for windy ridges
  • Charged phone + power bank
  • ID and some cash/mobile money
  • Small backpack
  • Any prescribed medication
